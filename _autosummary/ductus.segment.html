ductus.segment

ductus.segment¶

Cutting a text into the units that get scored – the segmenter= seam.

Two segmenters ship. paragraph is the default because it is the unit a reader actually perceives as “a part of the text”, and because scores over very short spans are noise. sentence is there for finer localisation.

Both are pure generators over exact character offsets: a segmenter never copies or normalises the text, so every offset it yields indexes the original.

>>> list(spans_of("One two.\n\nThree four.", "paragraph"))[1].quote
'Three four.'
>>> [s.quote for s in spans_of("A short one. And a second.", "sentence")]
['A short one.', 'And a second.']
